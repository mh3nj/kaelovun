The principle

Understand what you can.
Preserve what you cannot.

Kaelovun is built around one unusually strict rule: processing should never become an excuse for data loss.

01 / PRESERVATION
Nothing gets casually thrown away.

Licenses, documentation, fonts, references, unknown files, and anything Kaelovun cannot safely transform stay in the package.

Conservative classification
Original hierarchy retained
Unknown formats are preserved
02 / AUTOMATION

Give it a folder. Kaelovun takes it from there.

No “PSD mode”. No “archive mode”. Give it a file, folder, or archive and Kaelovun detects the situation, walks the structure, and chooses the right processing path.

Files and folders

03 / SAFETY

The original stays sacred.

Work happens in an isolated workspace. Verification comes before cleanup. Failure means preservation, not destruction.

Questions

A few important details.

Does Kaelovun modify my original files?

The intended pipeline works in an isolated workspace and only permits cleanup after successful verification. Originals are kept intact.

What happens to files Kaelovun doesn't understand?

They are preserved. Kaelovun follows a conservative rule: uncertainty is a reason to keep, not delete.

Can it process a complete project folder?

Yes. A directory is treated as an asset package, recursively inspected and reconstructed while preserving its meaningful hierarchy.

Can it process an archive?

Yes. Supported archives are extracted into controlled مworkspaces, inspected, processed, reconstructed and emitted as verified RAR5 packages.

Is Kaelovun tied to Evoury?

No. Kaelovun is standalone and consumer-agnostic. Evoury can consume its outputs, but Kaelovun does not require Evoury to operate.

Can I control preview formats and dimensions?

Yes. preview format and thumbnail format are independently selectable, with independent maximum dimensions and quality controls.

Can I choose how assets are named?

Yes. Automation derives names from the source asset; manual mode asks for a human-defined name, which is useful for mockups and curated packages.
